# TC-Facts-v1_2026_10_15

**Block:** Training Center Facts (Learner Support Desk)
**Owner:** Alhanouf Aldossary · **Version:** v1 · **Date:** 2026_10_15
**Source:** Day 5 Project Guide, Scenario B1 (invented scenario, no real organization)
**Use:** Paste the block below, unchanged, into the `[TC_FACTS]` variable of any TC template. It is the only source of policy statements. If a fact is not in this block, the templates must flag it as `NOT IN FACTS`.

```text
TC-Facts-v1 (2026_10_15)
F1. The desk is open Sunday to Thursday, 09:00 to 16:00.
F2. Registration closes at least 3 working days before a course starts.
F3. A learner can withdraw at least 5 working days before the start and receive a full refund.
F4. A certificate is issued within 10 working days after the course ends, for learners with at least 80% attendance.
F5. The desk cannot see the live status of a payment or refund. Refer the person to the finance office through the official channel.
F6. Inquiries are answered within 2 working days.
F7. The desk never asks for ID numbers, passwords or bank details.
```

## What is NOT in this block (never invent it)

Course prices and certificate fees · exact course dates, times or rooms · refund processing times · payment or refund status · links, phone numbers or email addresses · exceptions to any rule · names of staff.
